\problemname{Three-Sort}

Per-Magnus' sister, Lisa-Eva, has become extremely impressed by your programming skills.
So impressed that she intends to hire you as a consultant for a job.

Lisa-Eva has a triple of integers $a, b, c$.
The problem is that the numbers are not sorted!
This is of course wrong.
Your task is to sort the three numbers \emph{in ascending order}.
Your reward will be an imaginary cookie.

\section*{Input}
The input consists of three integers $0 \le a, b, c \le 1000$, separated by spaces.

\section*{Output}
You should print a line with the three integers from the input, in sorted order. The numbers should be separated by spaces.
